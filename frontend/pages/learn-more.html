Connecting Canadians to Family Doctors

CareConnect helps you find, book, and manage appointments with licensed family doctors across Ontario.

What is CareConnect?

CareConnect is a digital healthcare platform designed to help Canadians easily connect with available family doctors. We aim to simplify the healthcare journey by offering search tools, appointment booking, and personalized medical profiles.

Whether you're a patient looking for a family doctor or a physician managing appointments, CareConnect brings everyone together in one centralized, secure, and user-friendly system.

What You Can Do with CareConnect

  • ✔ Search for family doctors by name, location, or specialty
  • ✔ Book appointments based on real-time availability
  • ✔ Secure login and registration with role-based access
  • ✔ Get notified about upcoming appointments
  • ✔ Manage patient and doctor profiles
  • ✔ Receive email confirmations and reminders
  • ✔ View appointment history and cancel when needed
  • ✔ Built with secure, scalable cloud technologies

Our Commitment to All Ontarians

We’re building a platform that is inclusive, secure, and accessible for every individual—regardless of background, ability, or location. We aim to support the health of all communities through equitable access to care.
